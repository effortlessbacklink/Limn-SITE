Limn

About

Limn brings together furniture, fixtures, and architectural materials to help create thoughtful and beautiful spaces. The company works with hospitality, multifamily, workplace, and residential projects, offering both collection pieces and custom solutions.

Limn partners with architects, interior designers, developers, owners, and operators to understand the needs of each project. From seating and tables to lighting, millwork, flooring, decking, and cladding, Limn combines design, engineering, fabrication, sourcing, and logistics to bring ideas to life. Learn more about Limn at https://www.limn.us.com/.

Whether adapting an existing collection piece or creating something completely bespoke, Limn helps project teams develop spaces that fit their plans and express their brand. Imagined in California, designed in Milan, and made for you.
